# Diffusion in Solids: independent study edition

## Conceptual spine
Connect a conserved amount to a constitutive flux, connect flux to atomistic
jumps and thermodynamic forces, and determine which parameters an experiment
can actually identify. Reuse diffusion heat-treatment and diffusion-couple
problems while changing geometry, mechanism, thermodynamics, and measurement.

## Reader and prerequisites
A quantitative graduate unfamiliar with metallurgy. Assume differentiation,
integration, Taylor series, elementary probability, linear algebra, and basic
thermodynamic energy/entropy ideas. Derive the transport-specific PDEs,
transform solutions, random-walk relations, chemical potentials, frame changes,
and inverse procedures in place. Introduce crystals and defects explicitly.

R0: can manipulate calculus and probability but cannot distinguish material
diffusion coefficients or choose boundary conditions. R1: can derive and solve
simple diffusion models, connect jumps to diffusivity, analyze interdiffusion
and marker motion, design a measurement, and diagnose material-specific limits.

## Boundaries
This is original educational exposition, not a transcript or official textbook.
No claim of exhaustive slide/video transcription. Source frames are sampled
where necessary and that coverage is recorded. Empirical material claims need
directly inspected primary evidence. Synthetic demonstrations are labeled.
Python teaching simulations do not reproduce licensed DICTRA/CALPHAD databases
or certify industrial heat treatments. No fitted material constants are invented.
