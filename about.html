Sources and verification

An independent study edition built from the complete available English caption texts, selected source slides, explicit mathematical development, and scoped primary-source research.

Source and authorship boundary

The topic spine follows Mayur Vaidya’s Diffusion in Solids course at IIT Hyderabad. The NPTEL-NOC IITM channel hosts the supplied playlist; it does not change the teaching institution. The official 60-lecture sequence is canonical. The supplied upload order is preserved in the source manifest, and the supplementary course introduction is identified separately.

The prose, derivations, synthetic examples, code, and original diagrams develop the lecture topics independently. A timestamp supplies a navigation link to the related source discussion. It does not attribute every equation, proof, extension, correction, or numerical experiment in a section to the lecturer. These are AI-assisted educational notes, with independent AI scientific/editorial review; no human professional certification or institutional endorsement is implied.

Caption reading is not comprehensive audiovisual transcription. Caption errors can change signs, exponents, units, names, or a spoken correction. Selected 720p frames resolve specific ambiguities and are recorded in the bounded visual-inspection ledger. The source manifest keeps caption reading and visual coverage separate. Where a source slide is correct but its caption differs, the notes preserve that distinction. Unrecovered tables and ambiguous empirical phase identities are not reconstructed from plausible values.

Separate kinds of evidence

An amount balance is derived from conservation. A constitutive relation adds a physical modeling assumption. A numerical check tests an implementation or a specified finite case. An experiment supports an empirical claim under its own conditions. A successful macroscopic fit does not uniquely identify an atomic mechanism. The notes keep these roles visible when moving between continuum models, jump statistics, thermodynamic forces, and measurement.

Two independent full reviews are required for each accepted chapter, followed by targeted checks of repairs. Review records bind the actual chapter and scientific code/assets to content hashes. Source coverage, numerical execution, scientific acceptance, browser behavior, export integrity, and publication are recorded separately. Completion metadata and the reports under validation/ describe the current evidence.

Reproduce and extend

Open index.html after downloading the repository for offline reading. Local MathJax supplies mathematical rendering. Source recordings and external references require internet access. Each laboratory specifies its assumptions, inputs, outputs, and deliberate simplifications. Synthetic parameters are not material-property measurements. The reduced Python models complement the software demonstrations; they do not represent execution of licensed DICTRA projects or proprietary databases.

Chapter exposition is authored in HTML fragments. The public builder assembles navigation, code/output embeds, indices, and the reader without generating scientific prose. The portable rebuild does not require private captions, recordings, research captures, or working review files. Dependency licenses are retained in asset attribution.
