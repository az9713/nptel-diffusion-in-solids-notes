Separate kinds of evidence

An amount balance is derived from conservation. A constitutive relation adds a physical modeling assumption. A numerical check tests an implementation or a specified finite case. An experiment supports an empirical claim under its own conditions. A successful macroscopic fit does not uniquely identify an atomic mechanism. The notes keep these roles visible when moving between continuum models, jump statistics, thermodynamic forces, and measurement.
