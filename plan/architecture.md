# Course architecture

Twenty-eight chapters follow all 60 official lectures. Source-map groups retain their initial evidence identities; they are distinct from reader numbering.

## 1. Conservation, gradients, and Fick's laws
What must a diffusion model conserve?
Lectures: 1, 2, 3, 5
Check: finite-volume conservation, layered-slab resistance, radial profiles, and rotated tensors

## 2. Laplace transforms and diffusion initial data
How do initial and boundary conditions select a solution?
Lectures: 4, 5, 6
Check: ODE residuals, initial data, inverse-transform coefficients, and transformed PDE units

## 3. Gaussian and error-function diffusion solutions
How does source geometry change the profile?
Lectures: 7, 8, 10
Check: normalization, second moment, error-function boundary limits, uptake and inverse-profile recovery

## 4. Finite domains and reproducible diffusion computation
How can analytic and numerical profiles check each other?
Lectures: 9, 10, 11
Check: positivity, mass conservation, refinement against an exact solution

## 5. Crystal geometry, sublattices, and defect bookkeeping
Where can an atom move inside a crystal?
Lectures: 12, 13, 15
Check: site counting, charge/stoichiometric balance, and geometry

## 6. Vacancy populations, binding, and defect measurements
How can equilibrium defect populations and binding be inferred from measurements?
Lectures: 12, 13, 15, 16, 20
Check: free-energy minimum, missing-site balance, differential dilatometry and trapping-model identifiability

## 7. Random walks and atomistic diffusion mechanisms

## 8. Correlation factors and tracer memory

## 9. Temperature, pressure, and activation parameters
